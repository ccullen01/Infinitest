Frequently Asked Questions
How do I get an API key?
Get an API key from AI providers like DeepSeek, MiniMax, Qwen (Alibaba), or Zhipu AI. Visit their official websites, register an account, and you'll receive an API key. The app provides direct links to these services in Settings.
Is the purchase button not working?
First, ensure you have a stable internet connection. Try restarting the app. If the issue persists, it may be a temporary App Store issue - please try again later or contact support.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to manage or cancel your subscription. After cancellation, you can still use the subscription features until the end of the current period.
Is my data secure?
Yes! Your API keys are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server. All your question content and data remain completely private and under your control.
Why can't I generate lesson plans?
Lesson plan generation is a subscription-only feature. Free users can generate up to 3 questions per day and 10 per month. Subscribe to unlock unlimited access to all features.
Which AI providers are supported?
Currently supported: DeepSeek, MiniMax, Qwen (通义千问), and Zhipu AI (智谱清言). More providers may be added in future updates.

常见问题
如何获取API密钥？
从DeepSeek、MiniMax、通义千问或智谱AI等服务提供商获取API密钥。访问他们的官方网站，注册账号后即可获得API密钥。应用内设置页面提供直达链接。
购买按钮无响应怎么办？
首先请确保网络连接稳定。尝试重启应用。如果问题持续存在，可能是临时的App Store问题，请稍后再试或联系客服。
如何取消订阅？
前往 iPhone设置 → Apple ID → 订阅 来管理或取消订阅。取消后，在当前订阅期内仍可继续使用订阅功能。
我的数据安全吗？
是的！您的API密钥仅存储在本地设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。您所有的题目内容和数据都完全私密，由您掌控。
为什么无法生成教案？
教案生成是订阅专属功能。免费用户每天可生成3道题，每月10道题。订阅后可解锁全部功能的无限使用权限。
支持哪些AI服务商？
目前支持：DeepSeek、MiniMax、通义千问 和 智谱清言。后续更新可能会添加更多服务商。
